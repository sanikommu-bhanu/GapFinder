Product ecosystem

Fourteen surfaces, one loop

Every feature listed is implemented and reachable in the running app. They are grouped by the job they do in the loop, not by where they sit in the menu.
01

Diagnosis

Turn an attempt into a named, evidenced gap.
Check My Work
Photograph or type working. The pipeline reads it, verifies every line, and locates the first divergence.
/scan
My Learning Gaps
Open gaps, an error fingerprint of recurring misconceptions, and per-concept mastery.
/gaps
Analysis & confirm
Step-by-step verdicts, plus a confirmation screen when handwriting was ambiguous.
/analysis/[id]
02

Learning

Teach the one thing that broke — or help before anything breaks.
Ask a Concept
Any question routed to a curated concept, answered with a computed diagram, a read-aloud lesson and cited sources.
/learn
Solve With Me
For a blank page. Names the next move and why — the app never writes the line for you.
/solve
AI Coach
Retrieval-grounded, and can see your own record. Says so honestly when the context doesn't cover a question.
/coach
03

Mastery

Prove the repair holds when the help is taken away.
Practice & Teach-back
Targeted problems, then explaining the idea back in your own words against a rubric.
/gaps/[id]/…
Transfer
The same concept in an unfamiliar problem — weighted double in mastery scoring.
/gaps/[id]/transfer
Exam Mode
No hints, no feedback. Never claims mastery from one question; thin evidence returns “uncertain”.
/exam
Focus Mode
A timed block around one problem, with optional Spotify playback.
/focus
04

Progress & trust

Make the learner's own trajectory — and the system's reasoning — visible.
Learning Roadmap
What to learn next, ordered by what your own work showed, with prerequisites locked.
/roadmap
History & Full Report
Every analysis by day with its outcome, and a consolidated report across all work.
/history · /reports/full
Achievements
Five milestones tied to real evidence — gaps found, repaired, transferred, taught back.
/achievements
AI Observability
Every model call for your analyses: stage, provider, cache status, latency, errors.
/dev/observability

The shared spine

All fourteen surfaces read and write the same learner state, so a gap found in Check My Work is the gap the Roadmap orders, the Coach can see, Exam Mode re-tests, and the next analysis already knows about.
